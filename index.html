KSA Portfolio · command centre concept

End-state concept for a national real-estate portfolio command centre: one view across commercial, retail and residential assets with integrated CMMS, security and IT/OT, six asset twins and agent-led scenarios.

17 sites · 3 regions · 6 asset twins · integration fabric
How they're built. A venue brief becomes a venue file of levels, spaces, connectors and assets. A walk-network router works out step-free routes, one-way checkpoints and staff-only lifts, and a scenario pack scripts the day's events. The same venue file also generates a Mappedin MVF bundle for the Mappedin SDK and DXF floor plans for Mappedin Maker.
